Delete Your Account

You're in control of your data. Here's how to permanently delete your Mindora account, both from within the app and from this page.

1 Option A: From inside the app

Open Mindora → go to the About tab → scroll to the Danger Zone section → tap Delete Account, then confirm by emailing support from the prompt that appears.

2 Option B: From this page

Send a deletion request to support@mindora.app with the subject line "Account Deletion Request", from the email address associated with your account. We'll verify your identity and process the request.
